Settings

Proxy / VPN

Route all browser traffic through a SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy. Works with VPN providers that offer proxy endpoints (Mullvad, Proton, NordVPN, etc.), a local Tor daemon (socks5://127.0.0.1:9050), or a WireGuard/OpenVPN client exposing a local SOCKS port. Xer does not operate VPN servers itself.

DNS & ad blocking

Ad and tracker blocking is always on (Ghostery engine with EasyList and EasyPrivacy).

AI assistant

Install Ollama, then run ollama pull llama3.2 (or any model you like). Nothing leaves your machine.
